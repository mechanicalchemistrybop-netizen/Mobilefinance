            This dashboard was created with the help of AI, strictly for the author’s personal use. It is not financial advice, not a commercial product and not offered as a service.

            Your workbook is read in your own browser. Figures and projections are only as accurate as the data you enter, so please verify important numbers with your bank, broker or fund house.

            How to use Finance Dashboard

            Step-by-step guide · everything runs in your browser

            1 · Quick start

            1. Click the folder icon (top right) and choose your master .xlsx workbook. Password-protected files are supported; you will be asked for the password.
            2. The workbook name and the time it was last loaded appear in the footer.
            3. Pick a section from the left menu. Click any card, bar or table row to drill down; use Close or the breadcrumbs to go back.
            4. No workbook yet? Download the demo workbook below and open it to see how everything looks.

            2 · Top-bar icons

            • Folder – open a workbook.
            • Circular arrows – reload the current workbook after editing it in Excel.
            • Lightning – auto-refresh on/off (highlighted when on).
            • Eye – hide or show all amounts on screen.
            • Download arrow – install the app on your phone or computer (appears only when your browser allows it).
            • Cloud – back up to or restore from your own Google Drive.

            3 · What each section shows

            • Overview – net worth, assets, liabilities, monthly plan, insights.
            • Invest – balances by purpose, investment per financial year, maturity projections.
            • Loans – home-loan progress, interest paid, principal vs interest by FY (latest first), month-wise breakup, EMI and prepayment planner.
            • Cards – credit-card dues, limits and EMIs.
            • Portfolio – stocks and mutual funds: holdings, performance, 20-year wealth plan, live prices.
            • Calendar – regular investments and deductions; tick them off as they happen.

            4 · Loans: interest and prepayment planner

            1. Open Loans. Outstanding and EMI are taken from your workbook; type your actual interest rate.
            2. See the interest still to be paid and the closing month at your current EMI.
            3. Enter an extra amount per month to see time and interest saved. The table compares common amounts side by side.
            4. Click the Principal vs interest chart, then a financial year, for its monthly principal and interest rows.

            5 · Add a stock or mutual-fund purchase

            1. Open Portfolio and use the add-investment control. Search a fund or stock, or pick an existing holding.
            2. Enter date, units and price, then save. Entries stay in this browser until exported.
            3. Open Excel & changes, export the updated workbook, replace your master file with it, then reload it.

            6 · Backups and privacy

            • Calendar progress and pending entries are stored in this browser; use the calendar backup or the cloud icon to keep a copy.
            • Cloud backups go to a private, hidden area of your own Google Drive.
            • The dashboard never edits an open Excel file by itself; only the exported copy contains changes.

            7 · Install on your phone

            Open the hosted page in Chrome (Android) or Safari (iOS), then use Install app / Add to Home Screen.

            8 · Troubleshooting

            • “Workbook format not recognised” – keep the sheet names unchanged (Master file, Stock & MF Tracking, etc.).
            • Monthly loan rows missing – the Home Loan Transaction sheet needs a table with a Date column plus Principal / Interest (or EMI) columns.
            • Old numbers after editing Excel – save the file, then press the refresh icon.

            9 · Demo workbook

            A copy of the workbook layout (same sheets, headers and cell positions the dashboard reads) filled with random, fictional figures. Each download is different.

            Important: the dashboard does not silently update an Excel file that is already open in Excel. Export the updated workbook and replace the master file to make dashboard investment changes permanent.
